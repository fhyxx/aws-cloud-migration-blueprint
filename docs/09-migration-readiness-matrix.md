## Pricing and evidence rules

- Keep the saved steady-state subtotal at **$1,930.76/month ($23,169.12 annualised)** until a separately verified steady-state change is justified.
- Keep MGN, DMS, DataSync, temporary network, test/cutover and source/target overlap in the separate migration-period group.
- Do not treat MGN's 2,160-hour service-fee allowance as free replication infrastructure.
- The former combined DataSync assumption of 1,500 GB and $18.75 is withdrawn. Approximately 1 TB NFS is only an unverified source planning anchor; 500 GB S3 is target storage, not archive source volume. The $12.50 per 1,000 decimal GB NFS-only arithmetic is conditional and not a measured quantity or Calculator result.
- Do not assign DMS provisioned class, Serverless DCU range, AZ mode, storage or runtime without a compatibility check and representative benchmark.
- Record each evidence artifact with date, owner, source, measurement period and status. Redact credentials, account identifiers, public IPs and private customer data.
- Distinguish Calculator-verified amounts, independent arithmetic, derived quantities, provisional scenarios, pending items and formally approved planning assumptions.
- No AWS resource deployment or migration execution is implied by this matrix.

## Exit criteria for migration cost modelling

Migration pricing can be considered ready for a defensible estimate only when MGN source profiles and staging/test resources are sized; DMS compatibility and capacity are benchmarked; DataSync source bytes and final delta are inventoried; temporary network flows are approved; and the dated migration/overlap/retirement schedule is approved. Unknowns must remain explicitly unpriced rather than be replaced with invented quantities.
